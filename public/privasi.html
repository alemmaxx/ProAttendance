ProAttendance

Notis Privasi & Terma Penggunaan

Mematuhi Akta Perlindungan Data Peribadi 2010 (PDPA). Kemas kini: 20 November 2026 (v2).

1. Siapa kami

ProAttendance ialah sistem kehadiran, cuti, tuntutan dan gaji yang digunakan oleh majikan anda. Majikan anda ialah pengguna data (data user) yang menentukan tujuan pemprosesan data anda. ProAttendance (KUKUSAN LEGACY) memproses data bagi pihak majikan sebagai penyedia sistem.

2. Data yang dikumpul

  • Butiran pekerja: nama, ID pekerja, no. telefon, tarikh lahir, jabatan, jawatan, maklumat bank dan gaji (diisi oleh majikan).
  • Rekod kehadiran: masa masuk/keluar, rehat, kaedah punch.
  • Lokasi GPS semasa punch dan semasa "Update Kerja". Jika majikan mengaktifkan jejak lokasi waktu kerja, lokasi juga direkod lebih kurang setiap 2 jam semasa bekerja (bukan semasa rehat) dan hanya jika anda berpindah lebih 100 m. Tiada penjejakan di luar waktu kerja.
  • Swafoto semasa punch (jika diaktifkan oleh majikan).
  • Permohonan cuti, OT, tuntutan beserta gambar resit, dokumen HR.
  • Pengenal peranti (untuk mengelak punch dari telefon lain) dan token notifikasi.
  • Log masuk cap jari / Face ID: hanya kunci awam disimpan. Cap jari dan imej muka tidak pernah meninggalkan telefon anda.

3. Tujuan

  • Merekod kehadiran dan waktu bekerja.
  • Mengira gaji, OT, caruman KWSP/PERKESO/SIP dan PCB.
  • Memproses cuti dan tuntutan.
  • Pematuhan undang-undang buruh dan cukai.

4. Pendedahan

Data hanya boleh dilihat oleh majikan anda (admin, finance, penyelia mengikut peranan) dan anda sendiri. Data tidak dijual dan tidak dikongsi dengan pihak ketiga kecuali dikehendaki undang-undang. Data disimpan di pelayan awan yang selamat.

5. Tempoh simpanan

Data disimpan selagi majikan anda melanggan ProAttendance dan mengikut tempoh yang dikehendaki undang-undang (rekod gaji sekurang-kurangnya 6 tahun). Majikan boleh memadam data apabila tidak lagi diperlukan.

6. Hak anda

  • Melihat rekod kehadiran, cuti, tuntutan dan slip gaji anda dalam app.
  • Meminta pembetulan data yang salah melalui majikan anda.
  • Menarik balik persetujuan — namun ini mungkin menghalang anda menggunakan sistem kehadiran majikan.

7. Keselamatan

Sambungan disulitkan (HTTPS), akses ikut peranan, PIN / cap jari untuk log masuk, dan log aktiviti admin.

8. Terma untuk majikan (pelanggan)

  • Majikan bertanggungjawab memastikan data yang dimasukkan tepat dan mendapat persetujuan pekerja.
  • Kiraan gaji dan caruman adalah alat bantuan; majikan bertanggungjawab menyemak sebelum pembayaran dan penghantaran caruman.
  • Langganan diperbaharui mengikut pelan yang dipilih; akses dihadkan apabila langganan tamat.

9. Hubungi

Pertanyaan tentang data anda: hubungi majikan anda dahulu. Pertanyaan tentang sistem: KUKUSAN LEGACY (ProAttendance).

Privacy Notice & Terms of Use

In line with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA). Updated: 20 November 2026 (v2).

1. Who we are

ProAttendance is an attendance, leave, claims and payroll system used by your employer. Your employer is the data user that decides why your data is processed. ProAttendance (KUKUSAN LEGACY) processes data on the employer's behalf as the system provider.

2. Data collected

  • Staff details: name, staff ID, phone, date of birth, department, position, bank and salary details (entered by the employer).
  • Attendance records: clock-in/out, breaks, punch method.
  • GPS location when punching and when sending a "Work Update". If the employer turns on the work-hours location trail, location is also recorded about every 2 hours while working (not on break) and only if you moved more than 100 m. There is no tracking outside working hours.
  • Selfie when punching (if enabled by the employer).
  • Leave, OT and claim requests including receipt photos, HR documents.
  • Device identifier (to prevent punching from another phone) and notification token.
  • Fingerprint / Face ID login: only a public key is stored. Your fingerprint and face image never leave your phone.

3. Purpose

  • Recording attendance and working hours.
  • Calculating pay, OT, EPF/SOCSO/EIS contributions and PCB.
  • Processing leave and claims.
  • Compliance with labour and tax law.

4. Disclosure

Data is visible only to your employer (admin, finance, supervisor by role) and yourself. Data is not sold and not shared with third parties unless required by law. Data is stored on secure cloud servers.

5. Retention

Data is kept while your employer subscribes to ProAttendance and as required by law (payroll records at least 6 years). The employer may delete data when no longer needed.

6. Your rights

  • View your attendance, leave, claims and payslips in the app.
  • Ask for incorrect data to be corrected through your employer.
  • Withdraw consent — this may prevent you from using your employer's attendance system.

7. Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), role-based access, PIN / fingerprint login and an admin activity log.

8. Terms for employers (customers)

  • The employer is responsible for accurate data and for obtaining staff consent.
  • Payroll and contribution calculations are an aid; the employer is responsible for checking them before payment and submission.
  • Subscriptions renew according to the chosen plan; access is limited when a subscription expires.

9. Contact

Questions about your data: contact your employer first. Questions about the system: KUKUSAN LEGACY (ProAttendance).
