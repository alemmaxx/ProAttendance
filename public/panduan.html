Mula di sini (pemilik syarikat) · Pekerja — punch & permohonan · Penyelia · Finance / Boss · Soalan lazim

1. Mula di sini (pemilik syarikat)

  1. Daftar di proattendancemy.web.app → isi nama syarikat & kod syarikat (cth: ABC). Kod menjadi awalan ID pekerja (ABC001, ABC002…).
  2. Tetapan → Profil Syarikat: logo, alamat, telefon & warna. Logo & nama syarikat dipaparkan kepada pekerja.
  3. Tetapan → Waktu Kerja: jam kerja biasa, rehat automatik, OT automatik, templat syif (Pagi / Petang / Malam).
  4. Lokasi: tambah pejabat / tapak dengan GPS & radius. Pekerja hanya boleh punch dalam kawasan ini (jika diaktifkan).
  5. Pekerja → Tambah Pekerja (boleh muat naik gambar) atau Import Excel (muat turun templat, isi, muat naik). Beri ID & PIN kepada pekerja — butang Salin log masuk tersedia.
  6. Tetapan → Admin & Penyelia: jemput pengurus (finance) & penyelia. Penyelia tidak nampak gaji.

2. Pekerja — punch & permohonan

  1. Buka proattendancemy.web.app/worker → log masuk dengan ID + PIN → tekan Install App supaya ada ikon di telefon.
  2. Selepas log masuk pertama, aktifkan Cap Jari / Face ID — lain kali tak perlu PIN.
  3. Tekan butang besar untuk Mula Kerja / Tamat Kerja (ada pop-up pengesahan). GPS / QR / swafoto ikut tetapan majikan.
  4. Kalendar menunjukkan masa masuk / keluar & status setiap hari. Tekan tarikh untuk butiran.
  5. Ke mana hari ini?: senaraikan beberapa tempat / tapak sehari; lokasi semasa dicari automatik bila app dibuka (tekan + Tambah). ✕ untuk padam.
  6. Lupa Tamat Kerja? Sistem keluarkan automatik (hari rehat / cuti umum: biasanya 9 jam selepas masuk). Mohon Pembetulan Punch → Tamat Kerja dengan masa sebenar — OT dikira automatik bila diluluskan.
  7. Mohon: cuti (lihat baki), OT, pembetulan punch, pendahuluan gaji, tukar syif & Borang Tuntutan bulanan.
  8. Di luar pejabat? Tekan Update Kerja — lokasi & kerja masuk ke Jadual & Catatan.
  9. Profil: gambar anda, slip gaji (selepas diluluskan finance), status tuntutan, tukar PIN, notifikasi.

3. Penyelia

  1. Kelulusan: luluskan / tolak cuti, OT & pembetulan punch (tolak wajib beri sebab). Pembetulan "Tamat Kerja" yang diluluskan oleh pemilik / pengurus menggantikan rekod AUTO & mengira semula OT.
  2. Tuntutan: semak borang pekerja → Semak & hantar ke finance atau tolak untuk dibetulkan.
  3. Jadual: guna Jadual Auto untuk syif bergilir; tekan nama pekerja / tajuk hari untuk ubah sekali gus.

4. Finance / Boss

  1. Gaji → Tetapan Kewangan: kaedah caruman (jadual rasmi disyorkan), senarai elaun & KWSP/PERKESO, PCB setiap pekerja (Auto LHDN atau manual), kadar OT, kadar tuntutan & projek.
  2. Fail caruman (tab Diluluskan): PERKESO + SIP (ASSIST), CP39 (e-Data PCB) & Borang A KWSP. Isi No. majikan KWSP, Kod majikan PERKESO & No. E di Profil Syarikat.
  3. Gaji → Proses: pilih bulan → Jana Gaji → tab Semakan (Draf). Tekan Butiran & Slip untuk lihat kiraan automatik & pratonton slip setiap pekerja. Pekerja tidak nampak draf.
  4. Bonus / Insentif (pilihan): ikut bulan gaji asas atau amaun tetap, boleh ubah setiap pekerja.
  5. Lulus seorang atau Luluskan dipilih → slip dihantar ke app pekerja & pindah ke tab Diluluskan. Tarik balik jika perlu dibetulkan.
  6. Eksport (ikut senarai diluluskan): Excel gaji, fail bank (CSV), cetak semua slip; borang EA tahunan.
  7. Tuntutan: luluskan borang yang sudah disemak → Tanda dibayar. Tuntutan TIDAK masuk slip gaji; OT yang diluluskan masuk slip gaji.
  8. Langganan: bayar melalui ToyyibPay; resit boleh dicetak dari Sejarah Bayaran.

5. Soalan lazim

  1. Pekerja lupa PIN? Pekerja → pilih pekerja → Reset PIN. Atau guna Cap Jari / Face ID.
  2. Pekerja tukar telefon? Pekerja → Reset telefon berdaftar, kemudian pekerja log masuk di telefon baharu.
  3. Punch tiada internet? Punch disimpan dalam telefon & dihantar automatik bila talian pulih.
  4. Lupa punch keluar? Sistem keluarkan automatik (AUTO). Pekerja mohon Pembetulan Punch "Tamat Kerja" dengan masa sebenar; penyelia / pengurus luluskan — OT dikira semula automatik.
  5. Caruman tidak tepat? Pastikan Status (Warganegara / PR / Asing) & Tarikh Lahir pekerja diisi — kiraan ikut jadual rasmi & umur.
  6. Notifikasi tidak sampai? Profil / Tetapan → Hidupkan notifikasi. Di iPhone, app mesti di-install (Tambah ke Skrin Utama).

User Guide

Start here (company owner) · Staff — punching & requests · Supervisors · Finance / Boss · FAQ

1. Start here (company owner)

  1. Sign up at proattendancemy.web.app → enter the company name & code (e.g. ABC). The code prefixes staff IDs (ABC001, ABC002…).
  2. Settings → Company Profile: logo, address, phone & colour. Your logo & name are shown to staff.
  3. Settings → Work Hours: normal hours, automatic break, automatic OT, shift templates (Morning / Evening / Night).
  4. Locations: add offices / sites with GPS & radius. Staff can only punch inside these (if enabled).
  5. Staff → Add Staff (a photo can be uploaded) or Import Excel (download template, fill, upload). Give each person their ID & PIN — a Copy login button is provided.
  6. Settings → Admins & Supervisors: invite managers (finance) & supervisors. Supervisors cannot see pay.

2. Staff — punching & requests

  1. Open proattendancemy.web.app/worker → log in with ID + PIN → tap Install App for a home-screen icon.
  2. After the first login, enable Fingerprint / Face ID — no PIN needed next time.
  3. Tap the big button to Start Work / End Work (with a confirmation pop-up). GPS / QR / selfie per employer settings.
  4. The calendar shows clock-in / out times & status each day. Tap a date for details.
  5. Where to?: list several places / sites per day; your current location is found automatically when the app opens (tap + Add). ✕ to delete.
  6. Forgot End Work? The system clocks you out automatically (rest day / public holiday: usually 9 hours after clock-in). Request a Punch Fix → End Work with the real time — OT is calculated automatically once approved.
  7. Requests: leave (see balance), OT, punch fix, salary advance, shift swap & the monthly Claim Form.
  8. Outside the office? Tap Work Update — location & work go into Schedule & Notes.
  9. Profile: your photo, payslips (after finance approves them), claim status, change PIN, notifications.

3. Supervisors

  1. Approvals: approve / reject leave, OT & punch fixes (rejection needs a reason). An "End Work" fix approved by the owner / manager replaces the AUTO record & recalculates OT.
  2. Claims: check staff forms → Check & send to finance or reject for correction.
  3. Roster: use Auto Roster for rotating shifts; tap a staff name / day header to change in bulk.

4. Finance / Boss

  1. Payroll → Finance Settings: contribution method (official tables recommended), allowance list & EPF/SOCSO, PCB per staff (Auto LHDN or manual), OT rates, claim rates & projects.
  2. Contribution files (Approved tab): SOCSO + EIS (ASSIST), CP39 (e-Data PCB) & KWSP Form A. Fill in the EPF employer no., SOCSO employer code & E number in Company Profile.
  3. Payroll → Run: choose month → Generate Payroll → the Review (Draft) tab. Tap Details & Slip to see each person's automatic calculation & slip preview. Staff cannot see drafts.
  4. Bonus / Incentive (optional): by months of basic pay or a fixed amount, editable per staff.
  5. Approve one or Approve selected → the slip goes to the staff app & moves to the Approved tab. Withdraw if it needs fixing.
  6. Exports (based on the approved list): payroll Excel, bank file (CSV), print all slips; yearly EA forms.
  7. Claims: approve checked forms → Mark as paid. Claims are NOT in the payslip; approved OT is.
  8. Subscription: pay via ToyyibPay; print receipts from Payment History.

5. FAQ

  1. Staff forgot PIN? Staff → choose the person → Reset PIN. Or use Fingerprint / Face ID.
  2. Staff changed phone? Staff → Reset registered phone, then they log in on the new phone.
  3. Punch with no internet? The punch is saved on the phone & sent automatically when back online.
  4. Forgot to clock out? The system clocks out automatically (AUTO). Staff request an "End Work" Punch Fix with the real time; a supervisor / manager approves — OT is recalculated automatically.
  5. Contributions look wrong? Make sure the staff Status (Citizen / PR / Foreign) & Date of Birth are filled in — calculations follow the official tables & age.
  6. Notifications not arriving? Profile / Settings → Turn on notifications. On iPhone, the app must be installed (Add to Home Screen).
